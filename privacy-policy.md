10. MERCHANT RESPONSIBILITIES

10.1 Accuracy of Listing Details

Shopkeepers are solely responsible for ensuring that all product listings, including pricing, descriptions, and availability, are accurate and up-to-date. Hyperbola is not liable for any discrepancies in the information provided by the shopkeeper.

10.2 Obligation to Honor Listed Price

If a customer places an order at the listed price shown in the shopkeeper's storefront, the shopkeeper is obligated to sell the product at that price. This applies regardless of whether the listing price was entered in error, unless the customer is informed and agrees to the change prior to placing the order.

10.3 Price Discrepancy Resolution

If a pricing discrepancy is discovered before an order is placed, the shopkeeper must update the listing immediately. If the discrepancy is found after the order is placed, the shopkeeper must either:

Fulfill the order at the listed price, or

Offer the product at a lower corrected price (if it benefits the customer), or

Cancel the order with a full refund at the customer's discretion.Under no circumstances may the shopkeeper demand more than the listed price once an order is placed.
